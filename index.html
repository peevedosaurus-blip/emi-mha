Negate

量力 成美  •  Ryōriki Narumi

"Don't make it weird. I was already standing here." Narumi, after catching a classmate mid-fall and immediately pretending she had not.

Ryōriki Narumi (量力 成美), known by the hero name Negate, is a student in Class 1-A at U.A. High School and a former child actress. Guarded and dry, she hides behind an air of boredom that conceals how sharply she reads the people around her, and how much she actually cares about them. Her Quirk, Negative Mass, gives her flight, strength, and force manipulation potent enough that she spends more effort restraining it than using it.

Appearance / 外見

Narumi has a striking appearance that strongly influences the way others perceive her. Her features are soft and well balanced, giving her a gentle, approachable look that contrasts with the destructive nature of her Quirk. Her lips are naturally full and expressive, making subtle changes in her mood more noticeable even when she tries to keep her emotions contained.

Her pale blue eyes are among her most distinctive features. Despite their delicate appearance, her gaze is sharp and observant, often giving the impression that she is quietly evaluating the people around her, and when held for too long her attention can become unexpectedly unsettling.

Two beauty marks accent her face, one resting beneath her eye and the other just above the corner of her mouth. Her light ash-brown hair frames her face neatly and rarely appears disheveled, and her smooth, clear skin and naturally soft features add to her youthful look.

Although compact in stature, Narumi has a balanced, athletic build shaped by consistent training. Her legs are toned, and her slightly wider hips give her a strong but feminine silhouette.

Narumi is fully aware that she is attractive and understands how her appearance affects the way people respond to her. She does not depend on it, but she recognizes the advantages it can offer and is willing to use them when the situation calls for it.

Personality / 性格

Narumi tends to give the impression that she is bored with almost everything around her. She speaks selectively, reacts with little visible enthusiasm, and watches unfamiliar people from the sidelines before deciding whether they are worth engaging. In class she might slouch or look half-asleep while following the lesson perfectly well. The boredom is partly real, but it also works as a mask: looking unimpressed is safer than looking nervous, interested, hurt, or embarrassed, and if she acts like nothing matters, people have a harder time seeing what actually affects her. Years of acting and modeling left her especially sensitive to being watched, and although she can still slip into a polished, camera-ready persona, it now feels more like an old role than her real self.

Left to her own devices, Narumi is far more casual and tomboyish. She sprawls across furniture, sits on floors, and lives in hoodies, loose pants, and boots that let her move. She likes video games, hiking, climbing, wildlife, and physical challenges, and dirt, sweat, bugs, reptiles, and minor injuries rarely faze her. More often than not she is more annoyed by other people panicking than by whatever set them off.

She does not reject femininity so much as insist on choosing it. She genuinely enjoys skincare, makeup, jewelry, soft colors, and nice clothes, and her room still leans toward pale blues, clouds, and comfortable bedding. The point is that she wants femininity to be hers rather than expected of her, so she is just as likely to pair a delicate necklace with cargo pants or finish an elaborate skincare routine before pulling on an old shirt to play a violent combat game.

Because of her background she strongly dislikes being treated as fragile, decorative, or sheltered. Words like "princess" land harder than ordinary teasing because they echo the role she was pushed into as a child. She knows she is pretty and resents the assumption that it says anything meaningful about her character or ability. She no longer smiles just because someone wants reassurance, and her flat expression often communicates that people are not owed a performance simply for looking at her.

She can be sarcastic, bratty, petty, and deliberately difficult when annoyed, dismissing people with eye-rolls, muttered comments, and bored stares. Her insults tend to be casual rather than dramatic, delivered as if the target's stupidity barely required thought. She rarely goes looking for conflict, but arrogance, cruelty, or irresponsibility can make her genuinely sharp. She also knows how to weaponize the assumptions people make about her, briefly leaning into the sweet, harmless former-actress image with a soft voice and perfect manners just long enough to make someone else look unreasonable. That version of femininity is a tactic, not her default.

For all the attitude, Narumi is highly observant and more empathetic than she admits. She notices injuries, shifts in behavior, nervous habits, and attempts to hide distress even while seeming distracted. Because showing concern directly embarrasses her, her kindness usually arrives as action: quiet backup, spare supplies, food brought over, a practical problem solved, or simply staying nearby when a friend is upset. Praise makes her uncomfortable because it exposes the effort or feeling she was trying to keep private, so she deflects compliments with sarcasm, especially about her appearance. She would rather be recognized for competence than reduced to something attractive or harmless.

Negative Mass suits her physical, restless nature, and she loves the flight, speed, and freedom of moving through space under her own power. What frightens her is its destructive potential: she knows a misjudged strike or force transfer could badly hurt someone, so her restraint is equal parts responsibility and fear. She is comfortable putting herself in danger and much less comfortable being the danger, and she does not think she is weak so much as she is unsettled by knowing exactly how powerful she could become. When someone else is in immediate danger that hesitation tends to vanish, and she moves before she can calculate every consequence, throwing herself into unstable structures or between an attack and another person in ways that reveal how much she actually cares.

Around people she trusts, Narumi loosens up and becomes visibly more tomboyish, her humor rougher and her sarcasm less defensive. She starts inviting people to game, eat, train, or head outdoors with her instead of waiting to be included, and stops working so hard to make her affection deniable. At her core she is a guarded, sharp-tongued teenager who learned early that being watched is not the same as being known. She hides behind boredom because it costs less than charm, anger, fear, or sincerity, and underneath it she is casual, competitive, physically fearless, quietly protective, and far more emotionally invested than she wants anyone to notice.

Quirk & Abilities / 個性

Negative Mass (Emitter): though it is frequently mistaken for super strength, Narumi's Quirk is the manipulation of negative mass, a form of matter with an inverse reaction to force. When something with negative mass is pushed, it accelerates in the opposite direction to the force applied, much like the same poles of two magnets repelling one another. Narumi can convert parts of her own body, as well as any object she touches, into negative mass, and then dictate how that mass interacts with ordinary matter and force. Almost everything she does, from flight to her apparent super strength, comes from applying that single principle in different ways.

Overall Abilities

Narumi is a highly mobile fighter who controls space through flight and force redirection rather than raw brawling, saving her enhanced strength for decisive interventions instead of prolonged slugging matches. Her sharpest limitation is self-imposed: she habitually holds back, wary of how much damage her full output could cause, which leaves her powerful but frequently operating well below her true ceiling.

Strength

By routing negative mass through herself and her targets, Narumi produces feats that read as raw strength. A steady, continuous flow lets her lift heavy loads with almost no effort, while a sudden burst drives a forceful strike. Faced with something large and fast, she can grip it and weave a network, or "web," of negative mass through the entire structure so that its own momentum works against the web. In the textbook example of a crashing aircraft, she could take hold of the nose cone and thread negative mass through the fuselage, gradually slowing the plane's descent and stabilizing it without tearing the airframe apart.

She can also build negative mass structures inside her own body for added support and resistance to impact, and by adjusting the degree and distribution of that mass, for instance setting a region of her body to roughly minus two kilograms, she fine-tunes her physical responses moment to moment. At her current level she can lift or endure around 78 tons. Projections based on her father's Quirk suggest that, fully grown, she could handle roughly 314.15 tons, about the weight of a large airliner.

Flight

Narumi is subject to gravity like anyone else, but her interaction with the surrounding air is not ordinary. By converting the mass of the air molecules in contact with her into negative mass, she generates a repulsive force between herself and the ground, letting her levitate and fly. Holding that state requires intense concentration and precise control of the negative mass flux to keep her stable and on course, and a lapse in focus can break the balance and send her falling.

Her limits here are human rather than mechanical. She can climb to considerable heights, but her physiology caps her safe altitude at roughly 8,000 to 12,000 meters (about 26,000 to 39,000 feet). Past that, thinning air and falling oxygen bring on hypoxia and altitude sickness, with dizziness, nausea, headaches, and fatigue that can escalate to unconsciousness or worse without supplemental oxygen. Her Quirk grants remarkable freedom of movement, but it does not exempt her from the vulnerabilities of the human body.

Metabolism

Projecting negative mass is enormously energy-intensive, and her body is built to pay for it. Narumi's skeletal muscles stay highly metabolically active even at rest, burning far more energy than ordinary fatty tissue, and because that constant demand relies on steady protein turnover, her diet runs high in protein to keep her muscles repaired. Her cells also convert fuel with unusual efficiency: where a normal body yields about 2 ATP per glucose molecule through anaerobic glycolysis and roughly 30 to 32 through aerobic respiration, her physiology produces on the order of 16 and 304 respectively.

Sustaining that output takes an estimated 20,000 to 35,000 calories a day, which is impractical to eat as ordinary meals. To keep up, she works with a nutritionist on a specialized, high-protein, high-calorie supplement that delivers what she needs in concentrated form, backed by a diet rich in fats from sources like lean meats, fatty fish, and nuts. Before that regimen, she could not eat enough to meet the demand and regularly suffered fatigue and disorientation from caloric deficiency.

Fighting Style

Narumi arrived at U.A. leaning almost entirely on brute force, but as she trains she develops a hybrid style built around Judo and Karate that balances grappling with striking. At range she works from an upright Karate stance, using quick, powerful punches and kicks and staying light on her feet. When an opponent closes in, she drops into a lower, more rooted Judo base and turns their momentum against them with hip throws, sweeps, and trips, then pins grounded opponents with joint locks and submissions. The combination lets her pair her agility and power with a genuinely varied toolkit rather than relying on strength alone.

Genetics

Negative Mass is a genetic variation of her father Tatsuya's Quirk, Mass Absorption, which lets its user draw mass out of an object and add it to their own body to increase durability and strength. Narumi's version inverts that principle: rather than absorbing positive mass, she creates and manipulates negative mass.

Super Moves

Nullgrav. Converts the air around her into negative mass for near-silent flight and instant mid-air redirection.
Suspension Web. Threads a network of negative mass through a large moving object, like a falling vehicle or aircraft, so its own momentum decelerates it safely for rescue.
Backlash. Releases a sudden burst of negative mass on contact, either driving a heavy strike or flinging an attacker back along their own force.
Dead Lift. Runs a steady flow of negative mass through rubble or wreckage to cancel its weight, clearing tons of debris off trapped victims.

Weaknesses

  • Enormous caloric cost. The Quirk's energy demand is so high that, without her specialized supplement and high-calorie diet (see Metabolism above), the constant drain quickly leaves her fatigued and disoriented.
  • Constant precise control. Too much output can hurt bystanders and too little can leave her exposed, so she has to gauge the mass of whatever she affects with real accuracy. Even small miscalculations produce large errors, so she studies the typical weight of common people and objects to keep that margin tight.
  • Output scales with cost. The more negative mass she exudes, the more energy it burns. Heavier lifts and stronger opponents drain her noticeably faster, so drawn-out fights work against her.
  • Contact dependent and combat-raw. She has to physically touch what she wants to affect, so an opponent she cannot get a grip on, such as a body made of slime or water, renders her Quirk nearly useless. She also came to U.A. with little formal hand-to-hand training, favoring power over technique and often missing strikes, though this steadily improves as she trains.
  • Overuse. Pushing too far brings severe exhaustion, low blood sugar, disorientation, and eventually fainting. Exerting herself again after a fainting spell is genuinely dangerous and can lead to organ damage, coma, or worse.

History / 経歴

Early Life (Ages 0 to 5)

Narumi Ryōriki was born into a prominent Tokyo household. Her father, Tatsuya Ryōriki, was a mid-ranked Pro Hero whose Quirk, Mass Absorption, let him pull mass from external objects into his own body to boost his strength and durability. Her mother, Kiku Ryōriki, was a former marketing executive with sharp business instincts and a Quirk called Suggestion, which let her subtly steer the decisions of people who were emotionally vulnerable.

Narumi was a quiet but active child, observant, stubborn, and physically curious, more interested in climbing furniture and investigating insects than in being dressed up. Her looks and ease in front of a camera drew attention early, and Kiku began booking her for modeling jobs and advertisements while she was very young. A recurring role on a nationally broadcast children's program turned the hobby into a career, and Kiku gradually took control of her schedule, public image, and opportunities.

Her Quirk manifested just after her fifth birthday, on set. A loose lighting fixture fell toward another child, and Narumi grabbed them to pull them clear. Negative Mass activated across her body without warning, lifting both children several feet into the air and out of the fixture's path before her concentration broke and they dropped onto a padded section of the set. The fixture struck the floor where they had been standing. Neither child was hurt, but it frightened her.

Tatsuya recognized the ability as a mutation of Mass Absorption: rather than absorbing positive mass, Narumi produced negative mass within her own body, giving her flight, enhanced strength, and unusual resistance to force, which she could also channel into objects she handled. She went through medical evaluation, Quirk registration, and counseling, and Tatsuya taught her simple safety exercises. Kiku's main concern was the effect on Narumi's career, so the studio called it an equipment malfunction and kept the Quirk out of interviews.

Family Tensions and Divorce (Ages 6 to 9)

As the career grew, so did the friction between her parents. Tatsuya wanted her schedule cut back for steady Quirk instruction and a more normal childhood, while Kiku saw slowing down as squandering a rare opportunity. The disagreements increasingly centered on Negative Mass. Tatsuya treated it as a powerful inherited ability that needed careful training, while Kiku saw an unpredictable complication that clashed with Narumi's image, discouraging visible strength and reminding her that she was not the kind of girl who smashed things.

Narumi learned to separate her public behavior from her real personality, softening her voice for interviews and hiding irritation behind practiced expressions, while her tomboyish interests were treated as habits she would outgrow. Because filming made regular school attendance hard, she was largely homeschooled by tutors with no knowledge of her Quirk, and most of her real instruction came from Tatsuya in supervised sessions at secured agency facilities, focused on activation, hovering, safe landings, controlled lifting, and recognizing exhaustion rather than combat.

As the marriage deteriorated, those sessions grew inconsistent, and the couple eventually divorced. Tatsuya, who had scaled back his hero work after a shoulder injury, moved with Narumi's older brother, Asahi, to a quieter area outside central Tokyo, while Narumi stayed with Kiku, who held primary custody. Too young to grasp the full conflict, she understood only that her father wanted her to slow down and her mother believed he was trying to take her future away. She grew quieter, learning to read the mood of a room and hide what she wanted whenever she expected resistance.

Acting Career (Ages 9 to 13)

By nine, Narumi was one of the most recognizable young performers in Japan, appearing in children's dramas, family programs, advertisements, magazine campaigns, and supporting film roles, with a fast-growing social media following that Kiku carefully curated. She had private tutors, designer clothing, stylists, and expensive trips, but almost no privacy, and even her hobbies were judged by whether they fit her brand. Kiku encouraged singing, dance, and fashion while discouraging hiking, wildlife, rough sports, combat games, and hero training.

Visits with her father and brother were some of the only times she could relax, wearing loose clothes, playing games with Asahi, exploring outdoors, and continuing basic Quirk exercises with Tatsuya. The limited, inconsistent training also fed her fear of Negative Mass: she grew comfortable hovering and flying at low speeds, but was afraid to use real force against a person or object, and gradually internalized her mother's framing of the Quirk as something ugly or inconvenient.

Kiku praised her most when she seemed controlled, sweet, and harmless, and treated Quirk mistakes as evidence that she was being careless or ungrateful. That control rarely looked like control from the outside; it came through guilt, backhanded compliments, strategic tears, and reminders of everything Kiku had sacrificed, making refusal feel selfish rather than issuing outright orders. Narumi burned out, her private reactions going flatter the more exhausted she became. Her boredom was part exhaustion and part quiet rebellion, one of the few responses that could not easily be turned into content.

Breaking Point (Age 13)

The breaking point came when Kiku accepted a high-profile action film on her behalf, meant to move her from child performer to serious actress and to market controlled demonstrations of Negative Mass. Narumi saw the contradiction at once: the Quirk her mother had treated as strange and unsuitable was suddenly acceptable now that it could be sold. She refused the role and told Kiku she wanted to leave acting, attend school normally, train her Quirk properly, and prepare for the U.A. entrance exam.

Kiku accused her of wasting years of work and being influenced by Tatsuya, warned that the public would forget her, and insisted she was too young to understand what she was giving up, but Narumi did not change her answer. Kiku eventually relented, partly because forcing the issue risked their image, and Narumi moved in with Tatsuya and Asahi. Her departure was announced as an indefinite hiatus for academic and personal reasons, and she refused to comment on the speculation. Leaving did not immediately make her feel free; she stayed wary of praise and attention and carried years of fear about a Quirk she had never been allowed to explore.

Middle School to U.A. (Ages 13 to 15)

Living with her father and brother, Narumi started attending ordinary school full time. Her former fame made blending in difficult, with some classmates admiring her, some resenting her, and others trying to use her for attention, and she met invasive questions with bored stares or deliberate silence. At home she grew more comfortable, returning to outdoor hobbies, gaming with Asahi, and choosing her own clothes for the first time.

Tatsuya arranged more consistent supervised practice, still focused on safety and control: sustained flight, safe acceleration and braking, controlled lifting, rescue carries, landings, and stabilizing heavy objects through direct contact. Direct combat stayed her weakest area, since she had little martial arts experience and was uncomfortable using her full strength, often holding back and hesitating before a strike. Even so, she set her sights on U.A. Acting had taught her how to look heroic; she wanted to learn how to actually become one.

Synopsis / あらすじ

Entrance Exam Arc

Narumi arrives at U.A. intending to keep to herself and avoid unnecessary interaction with the other applicants. Her former acting career makes anonymity difficult, and several examinees appear to recognize her, but she ignores the attention and stays near the edge of the crowd, focused on the examination rather than the conversations around her.

The open combat format of the practical worries her. Although her training after leaving acting had been consistent, it focused on flight, controlled lifting, safe landings, and rescue carries rather than fighting, leaving her with little direct combat experience and uneasy about using significant force near other people. When the gates open she separates from the larger groups and uses flight to rise above the mock city, the application of Negative Mass she trusts most, surveying the streets and heading for quieter areas where she can use her strength with less risk of striking another applicant or sending debris into their path.

She relies on simple attacks rather than refined technique, using controlled punches, kicks, forceful pushes, and short aerial charges to disable individual robots, with Negative Mass both strengthening her blows and reinforcing her body so she can collide with smaller machines without the injuries an ordinary person would take. Even so, she avoids the upper limits of her strength and durability, aiming for joints, sensors, and unstable sections rather than trying to destroy a robot in one overwhelming blow. The caution makes her effective but inefficient: she often pauses to check her surroundings and gives up chances when examinees move too close, and the constant flight and reinforcement drain her, so her movements slow as the exam goes on. She earns 27 villain points, a total that reflects her limited combat training and reluctance to use greater force rather than any lack of physical capability.

When the zero-pointer appears, Narumi does not try to fight it. Her Quirk might in theory give her the strength and durability to interfere with a machine that size, but she has never tested herself at such a high threshold, and the crowded streets make a direct attack dangerous, since a failed strike or an uncontrolled collapse could injure several examinees. She moves away using flight to avoid the panicked crowd and falling debris, but during her retreat she notices an injured examinee trapped beneath part of a collapsed structure. She lands nearby, braces against the debris, and spreads Negative Mass through the section she is supporting, lifting and stabilizing the rubble without shifting the rest of the structure onto the student, then carries them clear while reinforcing her body to absorb smaller impacts. The rescue later earns her 20 rescue points.

She finishes the practical with a combined score of 47 points, 27 villain and 20 rescue, and passes the written examination to earn a place in U.A.'s Hero Course. The exam captures the uneven state of her early Quirk development: already skilled at flight and controlled rescue, but held back from the full potential of her strength and durability by inexperience and a fear of collateral damage. Declining the zero-pointer shows that she stays cautious at an unfamiliar threshold, while freeing the trapped examinee shows that she can act decisively the moment another person is in immediate danger.

Quirk Apprehension Test Arc

On the first official day of classes, Narumi joins Class 1-A and takes a seat near the back of the room. Several students recognize her from her former acting and modeling career, but she makes no effort to acknowledge the attention, staying quiet as the class settles and watching the early clash between Bakugo and Iida from a distance. After Aizawa arrives and takes the class outside for the Quirk Apprehension Test, her uneven development shows: she is comfortable using Negative Mass for flight, controlled movement, and rescue, but has far less experience with its higher physical thresholds, since her training had focused on safety and preventing accidents rather than exploring the limits of her strength.

For the 50 meter dash she uses a low-altitude burst of flight rather than running the full distance, staying close to the ground and slowing early to avoid overshooting the course. The cautious acceleration keeps her from challenging Iida for the fastest time, but she still places among the stronger students. She handles the standing long jump just as easily, using flight to clear the sandpit and land safely, since aerial movement is the most familiar application of her Quirk, though she avoids gaining unnecessary height or speed.

The grip strength test becomes the most notable part of her evaluation. Aiming for only a moderate amount of enhanced force, she misjudges her output and crushes the dynamometer before it can produce a usable result, surprising her classmates and revealing that Negative Mass involves considerably more than flight. She quietly sets the damaged device aside and apologizes to Aizawa, appearing irritated rather than alarmed, though the accident reinforces her fear that even a small lapse in control could damage her surroundings. Aizawa, who has already reviewed her file, recognizes that she was nowhere near her maximum strength.

During the repeated side steps she reduces her effective weight and uses brief lateral bursts to move between the lines, placing above average, though the rapid changes in direction expose her limited control over smaller, precise movements. She posts another strong result in the distance run by alternating between running and sustained low flight to spare her legs, finishing near the top of the class but slowing as the continuous generation of Negative Mass drains her. The seated toe-touch offers her Quirk no advantage and she lands an average result through ordinary conditioning, while a minor weight reduction pushes her above average in the sit-ups.

The ball throw becomes the clearest example of Narumi intentionally limiting herself. Despite the strength revealed during the grip test, she uses only a restrained amount of power and concentrates on controlling her grip, form, and release. The ball travels a respectable distance, but the result stays far below what her Quirk is likely capable of, and Aizawa recognizes at once that she held back, her reluctance to approach an unfamiliar threshold keeping her from showing the full potential of Negative Mass.

Narumi ultimately places fifth overall, behind Yaoyorozu, Todoroki, Bakugo, and Iida. Her flight scores her highly in the dash, long jump, and distance run, and her enhanced strength gives her exceptional potential in the grip test and ball throw, but her cautious output, limited experience with precise directional movement, and average showing in tests that offer no Quirk advantage keep her from ranking higher. Aizawa reads her restraint as both responsible and limiting: her concern for collateral damage makes her less reckless than several classmates, but her habit of suppressing her strength has left her unfamiliar with its upper limits. The test establishes one of her primary goals at U.A., learning that control comes from gradual experience with her full range of power rather than from avoiding it.

Battle Trial Arc

For the Battle Trial, Narumi changes into her hero costume and studies Training Ground B carefully during All Might's briefing, noting pathways and structural features without drawing attention. Teamed with Todoroki and Shoji, she adapts when Todoroki freezes much of the building, using controlled flight to hover above the ice and keep moving through the altered terrain. With Shoji relaying information and Todoroki locking down the field, she flies to the bomb while the opposing team is frozen in place.

Unforeseen Simulation Joint Arc

At the start of the USJ visit, Narumi stays near Yaoyorozu and Jiro while Thirteen introduces the facility. She looks bored, but she listens closely to the explanation of rescue work and the dangers of uncontrolled Quirk use, which echoes the safety-focused training she received before U.A., especially controlled lifting, stabilization, and protecting others from collateral damage.

When the League of Villains appears, Aizawa orders the class to evacuate with Thirteen, but Kurogiri blocks the exit, introduces himself, and announces the villains' intention to kill All Might. Bakugo and Kirishima attack him before either teacher can stop them, and Narumi follows a moment later, unwilling to let them confront the apparent escape route alone. Their strikes pass through Kurogiri's smoke, and Narumi stops herself from attacking once she realizes she cannot find a solid target beneath his body. Before she can work out how his Quirk functions, his Warp Gate scatters the students across the facility, and she lands in the Ruins Zone alongside Bakugo and Kirishima.

The three are immediately confronted by a group of villains. Bakugo and Kirishima take the offensive and quickly overwhelm most of them, while Narumi supports through controlled use of her strength and durability, shoving villains back when they move too close, redirecting falling debris, and reinforcing her body to block or absorb attacks that slip past the other two. Her interventions are effective, but she keeps using far less strength than she is capable of. Bakugo notices and accuses her of holding back; she ignores him and continues prioritizing control over aggression, while Kirishima recognizes that she is protecting their position and keeping loose rubble to a minimum even if she is not attacking as openly.

With the Ruins Zone cleared, Kirishima wants to search for their classmates, but Bakugo decides to return to the central plaza and take down Kurogiri, reasoning that cutting off the villains' escape route helps everyone trapped inside. Narumi, more concerned at first with finding the others, recognizes the logic of removing the person who separated the class and goes with them. They reach the plaza as Kurogiri tries to warp Midoriya away, and Bakugo pins the villain by the neck armor, exposing that Kurogiri's physical body is concealed within the smoke, while Narumi stays close, ready to step in if Bakugo loses his grip.

Shigaraki orders the Nomu to free Kurogiri, and when the creature suddenly moves toward Bakugo, Narumi reacts before she can gauge its speed or strength, stepping between them and reinforcing her body with Negative Mass to take the blow herself, with no idea whether her durability will hold. All Might arrives before the Nomu lands the hit and intercepts it. Once the danger passes, Bakugo snaps at her for interfering and assuming he needed protection, but she does not argue; she is more unsettled by her own reaction than by his anger, having moved without measuring the incoming force, calculating her output, or weighing the consequences.

She stays outside the confrontation while All Might fights the Nomu, and after Iida returns with the U.A. staff and the students are escorted out, she quietly replays the moment. Her training had taught her to treat strength and durability as abilities that demanded careful preparation, yet at the USJ she used both on pure instinct because someone else was in immediate danger. The incident becomes the first clear sign that her protective instincts can override the fear and hesitation that normally hold her Quirk back.

Relationships / 人間関係

Eijiro Kirishima

One of the first classmates to get past her defenses. His warmth and blunt loyalty remind her of her older brother, which makes her softer toward him than she would like. She still calls him annoying and rolls her eyes, but the words lose their edge. He includes her without making her explain why she wants to be included, and that makes him nearly impossible to push away.

Katsuki Bakugo

He unsettles her. Loud, aggressive, and exhausting, but also brilliant, driven, and completely unwilling to shrink his ambition to make anyone comfortable, which she quietly envies. Around him her boredom mask slips. He notices when she is holding back and pushes straight against it, refusing to treat her like a pretty, distant former child actress. It frustrates her, mostly because he is not always wrong.
