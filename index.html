Negate

量力 成美  •  Ryōriki Narumi

"Don't make it weird. I was already standing here." Narumi, after catching a classmate mid-fall and immediately pretending she had not.

Ryōriki Narumi (量力 成美), known by the hero name Negate, is a student in Class 1-A at U.A. High School and a former child actress. She hides behind an air of boredom that conceals how watchful she truly is, and how much she actually cares about the people around her. Her Quirk is Mass Inversion which gives her flight, enhanced strength, and exceptional durability, strong enough that she spends more effort restraining it than using it.

Appearance / 外見

Narumi is a very beautiful, petite girl who stands 154cm tall. She has fair skin, soft facial features, naturally full lips, and pale blue eyes. Her expression usually looks bored or mildly unimpressed. She has two beauty marks, one beneath her eye and another above the corner of her mouth. She has light ash-brown hair that is cut into a short bob with bangs and longer pieces framing her face. Narumi has a pear-shaped build with a small bust, narrow upper body, wider hips, and toned legs. Her figure is feminine but athletic due to regular training. She's aware that she is attractive and understands that people often make assumptions about her based on her appearance. Although she doesn't rely on her looks, she's willing to use those assumptions to her advantage when it benefits her.

Personality / 性格

Narumi often looks bored. But that doesn't mean she's completely detached from what's happening around her. She simply doesn't see why she should pretend to be interested for someone else's benefit. She's selective about who she talks to and usually just watches people for a while before deciding whether to join a conversation. Her answers can be quiet or blunt, so even when she's being neutral, she sometimes comes across as rude. She doesn't go out of her way to make people comfortable around her either. If someone decides she's mean because she didn't smile enough, she figures that's their problem.

Some of the boredom is genuine. Narumi doesn't really have the patience for annoyingly long explanations, forced cheerfulness, or people who turn everything into a performance. She gets irritated when someone talks just to hear themselves speak, and she has even less sympathy for people who make their bad mood everyone else's responsibility. The bored expression is useful, though. It gives people less to work with. It's harder to tell when she's nervous, embarrassed, hurt, or much more interested than she wants anyone to know. When something gets under her skin, her first instinct is usually to flatten her expression and act like she couldn't care less. But it doesn't always work.

Regardless of all the effort Narumi puts into presenting as aloof or bored, she's still just a teenager. Meaning she isn't as hard to read as she thinks she is. When she feels exposed, she's irritable. If someone actually manages to make her feel embarrassed, she tries to turn it around on them and act like they're the embarrassing one for trying so hard. The more cornered she feels about her emotions, the worse her attitude gets. Narumi especially hates when someone starts paying close attention to her and reading her a little too well.

Since Narumi grew up around cameras, she tends to give too much attention to things normal people wouldn't pay attention to. She is often correcting her own posture, checking her facial expressions, keeping her tone flat and neutral, and other mannerisms she believes people could read too easily. Simply put, she struggles with being perceived. It's just one of those things that came from being a child actress where most people decided what kind of person she was before she could speak for herself. Because of this, even positive attention can make her uncomfortable. Genuine compliments make Narumi feel a complicated mess of things. It's easy for her to dismiss casual praise. However, when praise becomes too sincere or hits too close to home on something that genuinely matters to her, she's left feeling exposed. In the same vein, she feels a little weird about people showing her genuine concern or worry. She doesn't always know how to respond when someone can see past the version of herself she puts out for the world to see.

During her work as a child actress, Narumi was often praised by adults for her naturally sweet disposition, but not for the reason most normal adults would. Narumi's nature as a child made her easier to handle and persuade, allowing them to shape her into something marketable. The main culprit being her mother, Kiku. Kiku knew Narumi's natural charm, femininity, and cute appearance could make it big if she secured the right opportunities. Her mother taught Narumi that her femininity could be useful from a very young age. Because of this, Narumi is particularly sensitive about being only seen in certain lights and not taken seriously. There have been quite a few people that have mistakenly assumed Narumi is nothing more than a girl who should stand there, look nice, and let someone else do the heavy lifting. Narumi doesn't have a poor relationship with her femininity. She likes stereotypically girly things simply because those are her interests regardless of how her mother often made it seem like those interests were the only things she should be interested in. She likes makeup, skincare, fashion... What she hates is the assumption that because she is beautiful and distinctly feminine, those are the only two things she's allowed to be.

Narumi would rather be praised for her work than her appearance. Compliments about her appearance aren't entirely unwelcome... But they really don't mean much to her in the grand scheme of things. Especially if that particular person only recognizes her beauty and not the effort she's putting into being a hero.

However, she isn't above using her beauty as a tool when it benefits her. There have been times where she has used someone's assumptions about her to play into them to get her way. If someone expects her to be sweet and harmless, Narumi may let them keep thinking that.

Narumi also has a petty streak that becomes more obvious once she's annoyed. Her insults aren't really all that elaborate. It's usually just a single biting comment before her patience completely runs out and she has to exit the conversation. She has the least patience for people who are arrogant, invasive, or condescending. At first, she'll usually try to ignore them. That's her default. Narumi really doesn't like wasting energy on someone who's obviously trying to provoke her. If they refuse to stop, however, she can become cruel in a very casual way. She's good at noticing what someone is insecure about and mentioning it as if the thought only just occurred to her.

Despite Narumi trying to represent herself as unflappable and like she's got some calculated social strategy in her back pocket... It's not always true. In fact, it's probably not true most of the time. Most of the time, she's just being a brat. There are times when she gets competitive over stupid stuff, pouts when she loses, and stubbornly refuses to admit when someone's right. Narumi is clever, but she's not some untouchable social mastermind.

She gets along best with people who are friendly without crowding her. Straightforward kindness works much better than fake niceness. She doesn't need someone to drag a dramatic confession of friendship out of her. She needs them to sit nearby, include her without making a big deal out of it, and give her enough time to decide that their presence isn't a threat. Even after Narumi starts liking someone, it may not be obvious. She can accept invitations, join group activities, and willingly spend hours with a person while looking almost completely neutral. People sometimes wonder whether she even enjoys their company when she's already started thinking of them as a friend.

Narumi notices far more than she admits. She can usually tell when someone is hurt, frightened, overwhelmed, or pretending to be fine. She just doesn't announce that she noticed. Doing that would mean admitting she was paying attention in the first place. Instead, she makes herself useful. She'll bring someone what they need, quietly take over a task they're struggling with, or stay nearby without demanding that they explain themselves. During training, she keeps track of her classmates even when she looks distracted. If someone is vulnerable, she'll shift her position without comment so she can reach them faster if something goes wrong. She doesn't like being thanked too earnestly afterward. A simple acknowledgment is fine. Anything more than that makes her shrug, complain, or insist she only helped because it was the easiest solution. Admitting she was worried feels much more embarrassing than putting herself in danger.

Emergencies bring out a different side of her. Narumi is normally cautious with her Quirk and spends a lot of time thinking about how much force she can safely use. That caution disappears surprisingly fast when someone else is in immediate danger. She'll catch someone who's falling, step between a classmate and an attack, or throw herself into an unstable situation before she's fully considered what might happen to her. She finds this part of herself frustrating. Narumi likes to think she's difficult to involve.

Her instincts don't seem to agree.

Mass Inversion has also changed the way Narumi thinks about her own body. She's small, but she knows how much power she can produce. Flight feels natural to her. She enjoys the movement, the speed, and the freedom of being off the ground. The strength and durability that come with her Quirk are harder for her to trust. She isn't especially afraid of being hurt herself. If anything, she's too willing to ignore her own safety. What actually scares her is hurting someone else because she misjudged how much force they could take.

Even a restrained hit from her can be dangerous. Narumi knows that, and it makes her hesitate. Early in her training, she often works far below what she's actually capable of. She prefers dodging, interception, rescue work, and controlled takedowns over trading heavy blows. She'd rather lose ground than realize too late that she used too much strength. When Narumi does stop holding back, it's usually for one of two reasons: she's taken the time to judge the risk, or someone is in enough danger that caution no longer feels important.

Academically, Narumi does best when a subject gives her something concrete to understand. Physics holds her attention more easily than most classes, especially anything involving movement, force, mass, acceleration, or mechanics. She's also interested in anatomy and the ways different Quirks affect the human body. She uses that knowledge outside of school constantly. Narumi estimates weight, considers how an object might shift once it's lifted, and pays attention to the way momentum changes her movement in the air. Understanding the mechanics of her Quirk makes her feel more in control of it. Mathematics and biology come naturally to her for similar reasons. They're structured, useful, and connected to things she can actually observe. Subjects built around memorizing procedures or repeating abstract information lose her much faster. English is easy for her because of her professional background and the amount of American media she's consumed. Unfortunately, easy doesn't always mean interesting. She has a habit of looking completely checked out, only to answer correctly the moment someone calls on her.

Outside of school, Narumi likes action-heavy video games. She prefers fast combat, difficult encounters, strong movement systems, and competitive multiplayer. She's much better than she initially lets on and becomes quietly insufferable whenever someone underestimates her. She can sit there wearing the same bored expression while beating everyone else in the room. Narumi doesn't share this interest right away. Gaming feels private to her. It belongs to family, free time, and the parts of her childhood that weren't meant for an audience. Inviting someone to play with her means more than she'd ever admit out loud.

Her sense of humor also changes depending on how comfortable she is. Around acquaintances, she's dry and restrained. Around close friends, she swears more, makes inappropriate comments, laughs when someone trips over their own feet, and keeps jokes going long after they've stopped being funny. She's more playful than she first appears. She just has to feel safe enough to be stupid in front of someone.

As Narumi grows closer to people, her attachment becomes more deliberate. She starts inviting them into her room, asking them to play games, or choosing the seat beside them without inventing some excuse for it. She still complains. She still deflects. She just stops working quite so hard to pretend she doesn't care. Getting close to people doesn't suddenly make her openly sentimental or endlessly warm. Narumi remains feminine, sharp-tongued, bratty, and intensely aware of how other people see her. She simply becomes more willing to let a few trusted people see past the expression she uses to keep everyone else at a distance.

Narumi learned early that being watched isn't the same thing as being known. The bored expression is partly habit and partly armor. Underneath it, she's intelligent, competitive, petty, observant, protective, and far more emotionally invested than she usually wants anyone to realize.

Quirk & Abilities / 個性

Mass Inversion (Emitter): Narumi's Quirk lets her alter the effective mass properties of her own body. By placing all or part of herself into an inverted-mass state, she changes how her body responds to gravity, movement, physical loads, and impacts. It manifests as three linked abilities: self-propelled flight, enhanced strength, and exceptionally high durability. These are not separate powers but the same effect applied in different ways. She can spread Mass Inversion across her whole body for sustained flight or large physical tasks, or concentrate it in specific areas for focused work, reinforcing her arms before lifting, stabilizing her legs while bracing, or spreading it through her torso before catching something heavy. Through deliberate physical contact she can also channel a limited version of the effect into an object she is gripping, carrying, or supporting, though that use stays tied to continuous contact.

Overall Abilities

Narumi is a highly mobile aerial fighter who uses flight, speed, and durability to move quickly between offense, defense, and rescue. Rather than remaining in prolonged close combat, she relies on sudden dives, interceptions, grappling, and brief bursts of enhanced strength to disable opponents or protect others. Her greatest limitation is her own caution. Because she fears injuring someone or causing unnecessary destruction, she often restrains herself and operates well below the full potential of her Quirk.

Strength

Mass Inversion gives Narumi strength far beyond what her natural musculature would allow, letting her lift, carry, push, pull, strike, and restrain objects and opponents many times her own size. Her muscles still initiate every action; she has to grip, brace, strike, or move whatever she interacts with, and the Quirk supports that movement by changing how her body bears weight and resistance. Distributing the effect through her arms, shoulders, torso, and legs lets her carry a load across her whole body rather than only the muscles nearest her hands, so she can shift debris, move damaged vehicles, hold open blocked exits, or hold up a collapsing structure while others escape.

Her strength can be applied with fine control. She can raise her output gradually while testing how something responds, which lets her move fragile or unstable material without immediately breaking it, and by channeling Mass Inversion into an object she is physically supporting she can spread stress through its frame rather than concentrating it under her hands, keeping large or awkward structures more stable as she lifts, carries, or repositions them. The same strength makes her an effective carrier, able to move injured civilians or unconscious heroes on her own body without placing them into an altered state.

In combat her strikes carry tremendous power. Punches, kicks, shoulder checks, tackles, and aerial dives can break defenses or throw opponents across a battlefield, with the Quirk feeding force from the point of contact through her hips, torso, shoulders, and limbs. Flight adds versatility, letting her add a dive's speed to an attack, change her angle before contact, or reinforce a throw, and she can halt her own momentum after impact so a committed strike does not carry her past the target. She also uses strength defensively, catching a limb, holding someone in place, pulling a person out of danger, or blocking an object from reaching another.

Flight

Mass Inversion grants true self-propelled flight, with no wings or external equipment. Narumi rises, hovers, accelerates from a standstill, and travels freely by adjusting how her body responds to gravity and movement: an even distribution holds a hover, increasing the effect drives her upward, and shifting its balance through different parts of her body moves her horizontally, turns her, or changes her elevation. Concentrating it through her lower body pushes her up, shifting it toward her back and legs accelerates her forward, rebalancing it toward her front slows or stops her, and an uneven left-to-right spread lets her bank and turn, giving her precise three-dimensional mobility over difficult terrain, between damaged structures, and onto targets from above.

She tends to fly in bursts rather than at one steady speed, rising suddenly out of reach, crossing short distances quickly, or intercepting someone before dropping back into a controlled hover, and her ability to brake and redirect lets her commit to high-speed dives without being locked into a straight line. Flight is central to her rescue work, reaching trapped people from above, carrying them out of inaccessible areas, moving between separate sites without waiting for roads to clear, and lifting equipment or debris into position. In combat it lets her control range and angle, attacking from different heights and moving quickly between offense and defense. Her aerial style is direct and practical rather than graceful, favoring sharp altitude changes, sudden turns, and quick stops, and treating the air as an extension of the ground she can punch, grapple, carry, and brace from.

Durability

Mass Inversion also gives Narumi exceptional resistance to physical damage. While active, it reinforces her body from within rather than forming an external shield, supporting her skin, muscles, bones, joints, and organs as a single structure so she can survive actions that would otherwise place impossible stress on the human body. This durability underpins her other abilities, since her body must withstand the weight of what she lifts, the recoil of what she strikes, and the acceleration of her own flight, and she can concentrate the reinforcement where she needs it, hardening her arms before a block, her torso before an interception, or her legs before a heavy landing.

It lets her endure powerful blows, tackles, falls, and collisions, brace against a charging opponent, and put herself between an attack and another person, and it makes her a physical anchor able to hold position beneath falling debris or support a structure while pressure comes from another direction. When carrying someone she can shield them with her own body while passing through debris or absorbing an impact, which makes her especially valuable in unstable rescue environments she can enter and hold open while others withdraw. Her durability is visually understated, with no armor, hardened skin, or protective aura; she still looks like an ordinary compact teenager even while bearing tremendous weight or taking a major hit, which leads opponents to underestimate the true scale of her Quirk.

Fighting Style

Narumi arrived at U.A. relying mostly on mobility, durability, and straightforward strength, but as she trains she combines her power with karate and judo. Karate gives her structured punches, kicks, footwork, and controlled targeting, while judo lets her use grips, throws, sweeps, and an opponent's own momentum against them. The mix makes her more than a simple powerhouse: she can strike at range, close into a grapple, use her strength to control an opponent's posture, and finish a throw from the ground or the air, and her compact size works in her favor when opponents misjudge how hard she is to move or how much force she can generate from a short motion.

Genetics

Mass Inversion is a variation of her father Tatsuya's Quirk, Mass Absorption, which lets its user draw mass out of an object and add it to their own body for durability and strength. Where he pulls in external mass, Narumi instead inverts the mass properties of her own body.

Weaknesses

  • Bound to her own body. Mass Inversion works on Narumi herself. She cannot emit it as a projectile, spread it through open space, manipulate distant objects, or make a force field, and she cannot repel or move anything without physically reaching it. She can channel a limited version into an object she is deliberately gripping or supporting, but only through continuous direct contact; it cannot jump between objects, cross empty space, or persist once she lets go. Any environmental damage comes from her physical movement and strikes, not an emitted force.
  • Extreme energy cost. Continuously generating and regulating the inverted state is exhausting, and depending on her training and Quirk use she may need 20,000 to 35,000 calories a day. Ordinary meals cannot cover it, so she relies on a nutritionist-designed concentrated supplement of calories, protein, fats, and electrolytes on top of regular food. Harder tasks cost more, and because all three abilities draw on the same Quirk, depletion weakens her flight, strength, and durability at the same time.
  • Constant regulation. She must continuously match her output to her speed, load, support points, and intended movement. Too much and she overshoots, strikes too hard, or crushes what she is holding; too little and she fails a lift, hovers unsteadily, brakes short, or lands badly. She studies the weight, balance, and construction of common objects, and in rescues has to judge whether moving one piece will destabilize the rest, all of which gets harder when she is injured, frightened, distracted, or exhausted.
  • Reliant on concentration. Flight and heavier reinforcement require her to sustain the inverted state; a serious lapse can throw off her balance, direction, or grip, and if she is knocked unconscious the effect ends entirely, which is especially dangerous in the air or while supporting a load. Wind, rain, poor visibility, and tight spaces demand more control, and the Quirk does nothing about her need for oxygen, so extreme altitude still risks hypoxia.
  • Objects are not made indestructible. Supporting something does not stop it from breaking. A vehicle, beam, or damaged floor can still collapse around a bad grip, and channeling the effect in distributes stress but cannot repair existing damage. She has to find stable lifting points, account for cracks and weak materials, and keep hold until the load is transferred to solid ground or another hero, since losing contact drops her support instantly.
  • Not total immunity. Her durability is strongest against broad impacts it can spread across her body. Concentrated attacks such as blades, bullets, needles, and claws can overwhelm a small reinforced area, and repeated heavy hits build up faster than she recovers. It also does nothing against heat, electricity, extreme temperatures, poison, gas, illness, or suffocation, and it fades as she tires, so a strong enough attack can break through. Because she is used to shrugging off major impacts, she tends to underestimate her own injuries.
  • Combat inexperience and bad matchups. Her pre-U.A. training focused on safety and rescue, so early on she leans on mobility and simple strength, telegraphs and overcommits, and hesitates to use real force against opponents whose durability she cannot judge. Technique improves with karate and judo, but intangible, fluid, gaseous, or slime-like bodies may not respond to strikes or grappling, and highly elastic or regenerative opponents can soak up punishment, since her offense depends on meaningful physical contact.
  • Overuse. Pushing the Quirk too far drains her energy and blood sugar, bringing hunger, trembling, headache, nausea, weakness, and poor coordination, then disorientation and loss of control, and eventually hypoglycemia, collapse, and unconsciousness. Continuing past that point stresses her muscles, nerves, and organs and can lead to seizures, organ damage, coma, or death. The danger is worse because she tends to keep going for others long after she should stop.

History / 経歴

Early Life (Ages 0 to 5)

Narumi Ryōriki was born into a prominent Tokyo household. Her father, Tatsuya Ryōriki, was a mid-ranked Pro Hero whose Quirk, Mass Absorption, let him pull mass from external objects into his own body to boost his strength and durability. Her mother, Kiku Ryōriki, was a former marketing executive with sharp business instincts and a Quirk called Suggestion, which let her subtly steer the decisions of people who were emotionally vulnerable.

Narumi was a quiet but active child, observant, stubborn, and physically curious, more interested in climbing furniture and investigating insects than in being dressed up. Her looks and ease in front of a camera drew attention early, and Kiku began booking her for modeling jobs and advertisements while she was very young. A recurring role on a nationally broadcast children's program turned the hobby into a career, and Kiku gradually took control of her schedule, public image, and opportunities.

Her Quirk manifested just after her fifth birthday, on set. A loose lighting fixture fell toward another child, and Narumi grabbed them to pull them clear. Mass Inversion activated across her body without warning, lifting both children several feet into the air and out of the fixture's path before her concentration broke and they dropped onto a padded section of the set. The fixture struck the floor where they had been standing. Neither child was hurt, but it frightened her.

Tatsuya recognized the ability as a mutation of Mass Absorption: rather than pulling in external mass the way he did, Narumi could invert the mass properties of her own body, giving her flight, enhanced strength, and unusual resistance to impact, which she could also channel into objects she was physically holding. She went through medical evaluation, Quirk registration, and counseling, and Tatsuya taught her simple safety exercises. Kiku's main concern was the effect on Narumi's career, so the studio called it an equipment malfunction and kept the Quirk out of interviews.

Family Tensions and Divorce (Ages 6 to 9)

As the career grew, so did the friction between her parents. Tatsuya wanted her schedule cut back for steady Quirk instruction and a more normal childhood, while Kiku saw slowing down as squandering a rare opportunity. The disagreements increasingly centered on Mass Inversion. Tatsuya treated it as a powerful inherited ability that needed careful training, while Kiku saw an unpredictable complication that clashed with Narumi's image, discouraging visible strength and reminding her that she was not the kind of girl who smashed things.

Narumi learned to separate her public behavior from her real personality, softening her voice for interviews and hiding irritation behind practiced expressions, while her tomboyish interests were treated as habits she would outgrow. Because filming made regular school attendance hard, she was largely homeschooled by tutors with no knowledge of her Quirk, and most of her real instruction came from Tatsuya in supervised sessions at secured agency facilities, focused on activation, hovering, safe landings, controlled lifting, and recognizing exhaustion rather than combat.

As the marriage deteriorated, those sessions grew inconsistent, and the couple eventually divorced. Tatsuya, who had scaled back his hero work after a shoulder injury, moved with Narumi's older brother, Asahi, to a quieter area outside central Tokyo, while Narumi stayed with Kiku, who held primary custody. Too young to grasp the full conflict, she understood only that her father wanted her to slow down and her mother believed he was trying to take her future away. She grew quieter, learning to read the mood of a room and hide what she wanted whenever she expected resistance.

Acting Career (Ages 9 to 13)

By nine, Narumi was one of the most recognizable young performers in Japan, appearing in children's dramas, family programs, advertisements, magazine campaigns, and supporting film roles, with a fast-growing social media following that Kiku carefully curated. She had private tutors, designer clothing, stylists, and expensive trips, but almost no privacy, and even her hobbies were judged by whether they fit her brand. Kiku encouraged singing, dance, and fashion while discouraging hiking, wildlife, rough sports, combat games, and hero training.

Visits with her father and brother were some of the only times she could relax, wearing loose clothes, playing games with Asahi, exploring outdoors, and continuing basic Quirk exercises with Tatsuya. The limited, inconsistent training also fed her fear of Mass Inversion: she grew comfortable hovering and flying at low speeds, but was afraid to use real force against a person or object, and gradually internalized her mother's framing of the Quirk as something ugly or inconvenient.

Kiku praised her most when she seemed controlled, sweet, and harmless, and treated Quirk mistakes as evidence that she was being careless or ungrateful. That control rarely looked like control from the outside; it came through guilt, backhanded compliments, strategic tears, and reminders of everything Kiku had sacrificed, making refusal feel selfish rather than issuing outright orders. Narumi burned out, her private reactions going flatter the more exhausted she became. Her boredom was part exhaustion and part quiet rebellion, one of the few responses that could not easily be turned into content.

Breaking Point (Age 13)

The breaking point came when Kiku accepted a high-profile action film on her behalf, meant to move her from child performer to serious actress and to market controlled demonstrations of Mass Inversion. Narumi saw the contradiction at once: the Quirk her mother had treated as strange and unsuitable was suddenly acceptable now that it could be sold. She refused the role and told Kiku she wanted to leave acting, attend school normally, train her Quirk properly, and prepare for the U.A. entrance exam.

Kiku accused her of wasting years of work and being influenced by Tatsuya, warned that the public would forget her, and insisted she was too young to understand what she was giving up, but Narumi did not change her answer. Kiku eventually relented, partly because forcing the issue risked their image, and Narumi moved in with Tatsuya and Asahi. Her departure was announced as an indefinite hiatus for academic and personal reasons, and she refused to comment on the speculation. Leaving did not immediately make her feel free; she stayed wary of praise and attention and carried years of fear about a Quirk she had never been allowed to explore.

Middle School to U.A. (Ages 13 to 15)

Living with her father and brother, Narumi started attending ordinary school full time. Her former fame made blending in difficult, with some classmates admiring her, some resenting her, and others trying to use her for attention, and she met invasive questions with bored stares or deliberate silence. At home she grew more comfortable, returning to outdoor hobbies, gaming with Asahi, and choosing her own clothes for the first time.

Tatsuya arranged more consistent supervised practice, still focused on safety and control: sustained flight, safe acceleration and braking, controlled lifting, rescue carries, landings, and stabilizing heavy objects through direct contact. Direct combat stayed her weakest area, since she had little martial arts experience and was uncomfortable using her full strength, often holding back and hesitating before a strike. Even so, she set her sights on U.A. Acting had taught her how to look heroic; she wanted to learn how to actually become one.

Synopsis / あらすじ

Entrance Exam Arc

Narumi arrives at U.A. intending to keep to herself and avoid unnecessary interaction with the other applicants. Her former acting career makes anonymity difficult, and several examinees appear to recognize her, but she ignores the attention and stays near the edge of the crowd, focused on the examination rather than the conversations around her.

The open combat format of the practical worries her. Although her training after leaving acting had been consistent, it focused on flight, controlled lifting, safe landings, and rescue carries rather than fighting, leaving her with little direct combat experience and uneasy about using significant force near other people. When the gates open she separates from the larger groups and uses flight to rise above the mock city, the application of Mass Inversion she trusts most, surveying the streets and heading for quieter areas where she can use her strength with less risk of striking another applicant or sending debris into their path.

She relies on simple attacks rather than refined technique, using controlled punches, kicks, forceful pushes, and short aerial charges to disable individual robots, with Mass Inversion both strengthening her blows and reinforcing her body so she can collide with smaller machines without the injuries an ordinary person would take. Even so, she avoids the upper limits of her strength and durability, aiming for joints, sensors, and unstable sections rather than trying to destroy a robot in one overwhelming blow. The caution makes her effective but inefficient: she often pauses to check her surroundings and gives up chances when examinees move too close, and the constant flight and reinforcement drain her, so her movements slow as the exam goes on. She earns 27 villain points, a total that reflects her limited combat training and reluctance to use greater force rather than any lack of physical capability.

When the zero-pointer appears, Narumi does not try to fight it. Her Quirk might in theory give her the strength and durability to interfere with a machine that size, but she has never tested herself at such a high threshold, and the crowded streets make a direct attack dangerous, since a failed strike or an uncontrolled collapse could injure several examinees. She moves away using flight to avoid the panicked crowd and falling debris, but during her retreat she notices an injured examinee trapped beneath part of a collapsed structure. She lands nearby, braces against the debris, and spreads Mass Inversion through the section she is supporting, lifting and stabilizing the rubble without shifting the rest of the structure onto the student, then carries them clear while reinforcing her body to absorb smaller impacts. The rescue later earns her 20 rescue points.

She finishes the practical with a combined score of 47 points, 27 villain and 20 rescue, and passes the written examination to earn a place in U.A.'s Hero Course. The exam captures the uneven state of her early Quirk development: already skilled at flight and controlled rescue, but held back from the full potential of her strength and durability by inexperience and a fear of collateral damage. Declining the zero-pointer shows that she stays cautious at an unfamiliar threshold, while freeing the trapped examinee shows that she can act decisively the moment another person is in immediate danger.

Quirk Apprehension Test Arc

On the first official day of classes, Narumi joins Class 1-A and takes a seat near the back of the room. Several students recognize her from her former acting and modeling career, but she makes no effort to acknowledge the attention, staying quiet as the class settles and watching the early clash between Bakugo and Iida from a distance. After Aizawa arrives and takes the class outside for the Quirk Apprehension Test, her uneven development shows: she is comfortable using Mass Inversion for flight, controlled movement, and rescue, but has far less experience with its higher physical thresholds, since her training had focused on safety and preventing accidents rather than exploring the limits of her strength.

For the 50 meter dash she uses a low-altitude burst of flight rather than running the full distance, staying close to the ground and slowing early to avoid overshooting the course. The cautious acceleration keeps her from challenging Iida for the fastest time, but she still places among the stronger students. She handles the standing long jump just as easily, using flight to clear the sandpit and land safely, since aerial movement is the most familiar application of her Quirk, though she avoids gaining unnecessary height or speed.

The grip strength test becomes the most notable part of her evaluation. Aiming for only a moderate amount of enhanced force, she misjudges her output and crushes the dynamometer before it can produce a usable result, surprising her classmates and revealing that Mass Inversion involves considerably more than flight. She quietly sets the damaged device aside and apologizes to Aizawa, appearing irritated rather than alarmed, though the accident reinforces her fear that even a small lapse in control could damage her surroundings. Aizawa, who has already reviewed her file, recognizes that she was nowhere near her maximum strength.

During the repeated side steps she reduces her effective weight and uses brief lateral bursts to move between the lines, placing above average, though the rapid changes in direction expose her limited control over smaller, precise movements. She posts another strong result in the distance run by alternating between running and sustained low flight to spare her legs, finishing near the top of the class but slowing as the continuous generation of Mass Inversion drains her. The seated toe-touch offers her Quirk no advantage and she lands an average result through ordinary conditioning, while a minor weight reduction pushes her above average in the sit-ups.

The ball throw becomes the clearest example of Narumi intentionally limiting herself. Despite the strength revealed during the grip test, she uses only a restrained amount of power and concentrates on controlling her grip, form, and release. The ball travels a respectable distance, but the result stays far below what her Quirk is likely capable of, and Aizawa recognizes at once that she held back, her reluctance to approach an unfamiliar threshold keeping her from showing the full potential of Mass Inversion.

Narumi ultimately places fifth overall, behind Yaoyorozu, Todoroki, Bakugo, and Iida. Her flight scores her highly in the dash, long jump, and distance run, and her enhanced strength gives her exceptional potential in the grip test and ball throw, but her cautious output, limited experience with precise directional movement, and average showing in tests that offer no Quirk advantage keep her from ranking higher. Aizawa reads her restraint as both responsible and limiting: her concern for collateral damage makes her less reckless than several classmates, but her habit of suppressing her strength has left her unfamiliar with its upper limits. The test establishes one of her primary goals at U.A., learning that control comes from gradual experience with her full range of power rather than from avoiding it.

Battle Trial Arc

For the Battle Trial, Narumi changes into her hero costume and studies Training Ground B carefully during All Might's briefing, noting pathways and structural features without drawing attention. Teamed with Todoroki and Shoji, she adapts when Todoroki freezes much of the building, using controlled flight to hover above the ice and keep moving through the altered terrain. With Shoji relaying information and Todoroki locking down the field, she flies to the bomb while the opposing team is frozen in place.

Unforeseen Simulation Joint Arc

At the start of the USJ visit, Narumi stays near Yaoyorozu and Jiro while Thirteen introduces the facility. She looks bored, but she listens closely to the explanation of rescue work and the dangers of uncontrolled Quirk use, which echoes the safety-focused training she received before U.A., especially controlled lifting, stabilization, and protecting others from collateral damage.

When the League of Villains appears, Aizawa orders the class to evacuate with Thirteen, but Kurogiri blocks the exit, introduces himself, and announces the villains' intention to kill All Might. Bakugo and Kirishima attack him before either teacher can stop them, and Narumi follows a moment later, unwilling to let them confront the apparent escape route alone. Their strikes pass through Kurogiri's smoke, and Narumi stops herself from attacking once she realizes she cannot find a solid target beneath his body. Before she can work out how his Quirk functions, his Warp Gate scatters the students across the facility, and she lands in the Ruins Zone alongside Bakugo and Kirishima.

The three are immediately confronted by a group of villains. Bakugo and Kirishima take the offensive and quickly overwhelm most of them, while Narumi supports through controlled use of her strength and durability, shoving villains back when they move too close, redirecting falling debris, and reinforcing her body to block or absorb attacks that slip past the other two. Her interventions are effective, but she keeps using far less strength than she is capable of. Bakugo notices and accuses her of holding back; she ignores him and continues prioritizing control over aggression, while Kirishima recognizes that she is protecting their position and keeping loose rubble to a minimum even if she is not attacking as openly.

With the Ruins Zone cleared, Kirishima wants to search for their classmates, but Bakugo decides to return to the central plaza and take down Kurogiri, reasoning that cutting off the villains' escape route helps everyone trapped inside. Narumi, more concerned at first with finding the others, recognizes the logic of removing the person who separated the class and goes with them. They reach the plaza as Kurogiri tries to warp Midoriya away, and Bakugo pins the villain by the neck armor, exposing that Kurogiri's physical body is concealed within the smoke, while Narumi stays close, ready to step in if Bakugo loses his grip.

Shigaraki orders the Nomu to free Kurogiri, and when the creature suddenly moves toward Bakugo, Narumi reacts before she can gauge its speed or strength, stepping between them and reinforcing her body with Mass Inversion to take the blow herself, with no idea whether her durability will hold. All Might arrives before the Nomu lands the hit and intercepts it. Once the danger passes, Bakugo snaps at her for interfering and assuming he needed protection, but she does not argue; she is more unsettled by her own reaction than by his anger, having moved without measuring the incoming force, calculating her output, or weighing the consequences.

She stays outside the confrontation while All Might fights the Nomu, and after Iida returns with the U.A. staff and the students are escorted out, she quietly replays the moment. Her training had taught her to treat strength and durability as abilities that demanded careful preparation, yet at the USJ she used both on pure instinct because someone else was in immediate danger. The incident becomes the first clear sign that her protective instincts can override the fear and hesitation that normally hold her Quirk back.

U.A. Sports Festival Arc

Two days after the U.S.J. attack, Class 1-A returns to school. The others spend the morning on the villains and the media coverage, but Narumi says almost nothing, still unsettled by having stepped between Bakugo and the Nomu without thinking and with no intention of discussing it. When Kirishima asks if she is all right, she just says she is tired. Ochaco's sudden enthusiasm catches her off guard, and when Ochaco pushes the class to declare they will do their best, Narumi joins in at a fraction of everyone else's volume, supportive of the determination but not enough to celebrate it the way Ochaco does. Aizawa, heavily bandaged, announces that the U.A. Sports Festival will go ahead as planned, calling it one of the biggest chances for students to draw the attention of Pro Heroes, and arguing that canceling it would only make U.A. look intimidated. Narumi understands the logic, but the idea of returning to national television unsettles her, since it will be her first large broadcast since she left acting and she wants to be seen as a hero student rather than a former child actress. At the end of the day other departments crowd the exit to size up the class that survived the U.S.J.; Bakugo insults all of them, and while Narumi thinks he has needlessly made enemies of nearly the whole first year, she understands why Kirishima responds to him, since he says exactly what he means to do without softening it for anyone.

Preparation

Over the next two weeks she trains mostly on mobility: sustained flight, hard braking, sharp turns, controlled bursts of acceleration, and lifting and carrying heavy objects in the air, expecting obstacles that demand both movement and strength. She spends far less time on direct combat, still uncomfortable sparring at higher output and mostly striking reinforced targets rather than classmates. Tatsuya encourages her to treat the Festival as a chance to establish the range she can use safely rather than a test of her limits, which she agrees with, though she is more focused on not making a public mistake than on finding out how strong she really is.

Obstacle Race

On the day itself, the stadium cameras immediately change how she carries herself, straightening her posture and pulling her back into her polished public manner. When Bakugo uses the athlete pledge to declare he will win, angering the other competitors, she mutters that he has somehow made things worse. The first event is a four-kilometer obstacle race. Todoroki freezes the exit at the start, and Narumi lifts above the ice, hugging the ceiling of the passage until space opens ahead. At the robot obstacle she flies through gaps rather than bringing down a Zero-Pointer, unwilling to use that much force with so many students nearby, disabling smaller machines and catching a falling chunk of robot before it can hit the students behind her, which costs her several seconds but keeps the route open. She clears The Fall easily by flying over the ravine, and stays above the minefield without going at full speed while others are launched around unpredictably. Iida passes her near the end, and she slows early to avoid overshooting the line, finishing fifth behind Midoriya, Todoroki, Bakugo, and Iida. The observing heroes note her flight, the contrast between her small frame and the strength she showed moving the debris, and the time she gave up to keep others safe. Fifth place also makes her one of the more valuable targets in the next event.

Cavalry Battle

The second event is a fifteen-minute Cavalry Battle. Narumi forms a team with Shoji, Tsuyu, and Mineta, clearly annoyed at the last addition even as she admits his Quirk is useful. As the rider, she hovers just above her teammates' shoulders to take weight off them, which makes the team faster and easier to steer. Team Ryōriki avoids the strongest teams, ignores Midoriya's ten-million-point headband, and picks off lower-value teams that drift away from the main fight, with Mineta's sticky spheres forcing pursuers to back off. They lose one headband but take several smaller ones, and narrowly dodge Team Todoroki's ice-and-electricity combination after Shoji warns that Kaminari is charging a wide discharge. In the final minute she focuses on protecting their remaining points rather than joining the scramble between the top teams, and they finish fifth to advance.

Tournament

Twenty-four students move on to a block tournament. During the recreational break beforehand, Kaminari and Mineta trick the girls of 1-A into cheerleading uniforms by claiming the school requested it; Narumi is less bothered by the outfit than by having walked in front of a national audience without checking the request, and stares Kaminari down until he apologizes.

Her first match is against Daichi Mogura, whose Quirk, Burrow, lets him tunnel and track movement by vibration. She hovers so he cannot feel her through the ground, refuses to strike him while he is half-buried to avoid a neck or spinal injury, and finally baits him by touching down near the edge before lifting away; he bursts up at full speed, misjudges the boundary, and surfaces outside the ring for a ring-out. She hovers over the hole and asks whether that even counts, landing more annoyed than proud, though the heroes note that she read his tracking and won without hurting him.

In another opening-round match, Bakugo faces Ochaco. The crowd boos him for blasting her back as she keeps rushing in, but he keeps his distance because a single touch from her could take away his control of the fight, and he is one of the few people treating her as a serious opponent rather than a fragile girl who needs protecting; despite what she thinks of him at this point, Narumi respects that. Ochaco eventually reveals that his repeated explosions supplied the debris for her Meteor Shower, dropping the rubble on him and charging while he is distracted, but he destroys the falling debris with a single blast and she collapses from her injuries, giving him the win. Narumi is disappointed by the result but respects the planning behind the attack.

In the block semifinal she faces Nao Kuroda, whose Quirk, Motor Lock, interrupts muscle control through sustained skin contact. Unwilling to risk a hard strike against an opponent whose durability she cannot judge, she decides to restrain Kuroda and carry them out instead. She lands and grabs Kuroda's forearm, but Kuroda catches her exposed wrist, and the paralysis spreads through her hand, arm, and shoulder. Her takeoff comes out uneven as one side of her body stops responding; she tilts, drops back onto the arena, and Kuroda holds on long enough for the effect to reach her torso and a leg. She stays conscious and can still feel Mass Inversion active, but can no longer coordinate it, and Kuroda guides her across the boundary for the win. Her durability does nothing here, since Motor Lock does not injure her so much as take away control, and Aizawa later puts it plainly: she treated getting a grip as the end of the exchange without thinking about what that contact let her opponent do.

More embarrassed than hurt once the paralysis wears off, she returns to the stands. Kirishima congratulates her for how far she got, but she brushes it off, saying she grabbed someone whose Quirk needed contact and does not deserve praise for it. Bakugo is blunter, pointing out that she handed them her arm; when she says she noticed, he tells her not to do it again, which she prefers to reassurance, since he treats the loss as a fixable mistake rather than proof she is weak.

Remaining Matches

After her defeat, Midoriya faces Todoroki. Narumi is disturbed by the extent of Midoriya's self-inflicted injuries, but his insistence that Todoroki's fire belongs to Todoroki and not Endeavor stays with her. She does not know the details of Todoroki's childhood, but she understands what it means for someone else to decide what a person's Quirk should represent, because Kiku spent years treating Mass Inversion as something embarrassing until she found a way to market it, so Todoroki's struggle to claim his own power feels uncomfortably familiar. Todoroki eventually uses his fire and defeats Midoriya in their final clash.

Elsewhere, Iida uses Recipro Burst to push Ibara out before she can seize control of the arena, and Tokoyami overwhelms Mina with Dark Shadow and forces her out of bounds. In the last major block match, Narumi hopes Kirishima will beat Bakugo even though she knows Bakugo is the more experienced fighter; Kirishima pressures him with Hardening and cuts his cheek, but Bakugo works out that repeated explosions wear his defense down and overwhelms him with a sustained barrage. Narumi brings Kirishima water afterward and tells him he nearly broke Bakugo's face, which he takes as praise. Kuroda then meets Iida in the block final, but having watched Narumi's loss, Iida avoids prolonged contact, secures Kuroda with Recipro Burst without letting Motor Lock take hold, and carries them out to win the block.

In the championship semifinals, Iida nearly carries Todoroki out with Recipro Burst before Todoroki freezes his mufflers and immobilizes him, and Bakugo picks apart Tokoyami by using flashes of light to weaken Dark Shadow before forcing a surrender with Stun Grenade. It irritates Narumi how naturally Bakugo gathers information by engaging, attacking and adjusting instead of waiting until he is certain. In the final, Bakugo repeatedly demands that Todoroki use his fire, wanting a win that proves he beat his opponent at full strength; Todoroki briefly ignites before letting the flames die, and Bakugo blasts him out with Howitzer Impact only to realize Todoroki never committed to the clash. He becomes furious and has to be restrained by Midnight before he can continue.

Aftermath

At the closing ceremony Tokoyami takes third, Todoroki second, and Bakugo first, though Bakugo spends it chained and muzzled to the podium over his outburst; Kirishima worries the restraints are excessive, and Narumi says the muzzle should probably stay. She wins no medal, but her performance draws heavy professional attention for her aerial mobility, her awareness and teamwork in the Cavalry Battle, and the tactical intelligence and untapped physical ability in her matches, alongside clear notes that she leans too heavily on caution and lacks close-range experience against opponents she cannot safely read.

The media treats the whole thing as her return to television rather than the start of a hero career, fixating on her looks and her acting past against the power of Mass Inversion, which she dislikes almost entirely. Tatsuya and Asahi congratulate her, Tatsuya especially glad she used her Quirk publicly without letting fear stop her, though she argues that being carried out of bounds while paralyzed is nothing to celebrate. Kiku messages her about how well she looked on camera and suggests she get professional representation before the next event; Narumi does not respond. Over the two days away from school she reviews the footage of her loss again and again, recognizing that she had assumed her strength and durability would make close contact safe. The Festival forces her to face the gap between physical power and combat competence, since Mass Inversion can keep her airborne, move tremendous weight, and shrug off major impacts but cannot replace experience, technique, or knowledge of an opponent's Quirk. It also changes how she sees Bakugo: still abrasive and needlessly aggressive, but she can no longer dismiss the intelligence in how he tests, observes, and adapts, which stands in direct contrast to her habit of waiting for complete certainty before acting. By the end she is far more visible than she wanted, but she has also proven herself one of the strongest and most versatile students in the first year, even if she is dissatisfied with how much of that she actually showed. Her final results are fifth in the obstacle race, fifth in the Cavalry Battle, and a qualifying-block semifinal finish in the tournament.

Vs. Hero Killer Arc

Two days after the Sports Festival, Class 1-A returns to U.A., still dealing with the attention the event generated. Aizawa has the class use their Hero Informatics period to choose professional hero names and reveals the internship nominations sent in by Pro Heroes who watched the Festival. Narumi receives 52 offers, a respectable number but well below the students who dominated the event, with most of the interest coming from agencies focused on rescue, mobility, urban response, and close-range combat. Several are clearly drawn to the publicity around her former acting career, which immediately puts her off them, and being sized up by dozens of adults she has never met reminds her uncomfortably of the entertainment industry.

Hero Name

When Midnight arrives to supervise the hero-name selections, Narumi is noticeably less enthusiastic than her classmates, though not because she lacks a name; she had settled on Negate before the exercise even began. What she dislikes is having to stand up and announce something she chose for herself, which feels stranger and more personal than performing from a script for a camera. When her turn comes she holds up the board reading NEGATE and keeps it short, introducing herself as the Mass Inversion Hero, Negate, which Midnight approves as simple and memorable before Narumi returns straight to her seat. Her composure nearly cracks later when Bakugo presents "Lord Explosion Murder"; Midnight rejects it, but Narumi has already turned away, shoulders shaking as she presses her lips together and stares at the wall, refusing to look at him until she is sure she can keep a straight face.

Choosing Gunhead

Given the week to pick from her internship options, Narumi spends more time eliminating choices than weighing them, cutting anything publicity-heavy almost immediately and taking a real interest in rescue agencies that understand how useful flight, durability, and strength are in collapsed structures or large accidents. Gunhead's offer stands out for a different reason: her loss at the Sports Festival made one weakness obvious, that she is powerful at close range but not actually good at fighting there, since lifting, throwing, or hitting hard enough to end a fight are useless when she does not trust herself to use that much force. Because Gunhead specializes in controlling criminals through close combat rather than raw power, she chooses his agency, and soon learns Ochaco made the same call for a parallel reason, wanting real combat understanding after her fight with Bakugo rather than focusing only on rescue. Narumi tells her it makes sense and leaves it at that.

Gunhead's Agency

On the first day, Gunhead walks them through the agency's crime-control work and the reports and coordination that follow each job. Narumi had expected him to be intimidating; instead he is friendly, patient, and prone to oddly cute gestures given his appearance, and Ochaco seems just as surprised. Their first day is patrol and observation, and though Narumi is comfortable scanning from above, Gunhead keeps her at street level so she can learn how criminals behave and how fast a situation turns into a close-range fight.

During an early session, Ochaco finally admits something that has bothered her since the Festival: watching Narumi fly, move heavy objects, and shrug off attacks, she had assumed Mass Inversion was just a stronger version of Zero Gravity, which quietly made her insecure about what she could offer that Narumi could not. Narumi looks genuinely confused and says they are not really alike, explaining that Mass Inversion mostly changes how her own body behaves, so she still has to physically carry or support almost everything, while Zero Gravity works directly on other targets, letting Ochaco touch an object, remove its weight, and move on to affect a whole area at once. In her usual bored tone she admits she is actually kind of jealous, pointing out that in a collapse Ochaco could make whole sections of debris weightless for others to move while Narumi would have to get under the rubble and haul it herself, and adds that Ochaco can do far more with hers while her own Quirk just makes her really hard to kill, which is enough to make Ochaco laugh. The conversation eases the awkwardness between them, and both stop treating their Quirks as competing versions of the same thing.

Close-Range Training

Gunhead quickly sees that the two girls have almost opposite problems: Ochaco needs confidence closing in because her Quirk is devastating once she lands a touch, while Narumi has no shortage of power but no confidence using it on a person at all. Paired with Ochaco, Narumi turns overly cautious the instant they grapple, securing a wrist and then hesitating, loosening a hold too early, or stopping a takedown for fear of too much force, until Ochaco snaps that she is not made of glass; Narumi's flat "I know" does not really answer the point. Gunhead has her train with Mass Inversion kept at very low output, forcing her to focus on stance, leverage, balance, and controlling another person's movement, which is deeply uncomfortable at first because she has spent years thinking about how much strength she can safely use rather than how little she actually needs. He teaches her to escape grips, break balance, control the wrist and elbow, throw, and pin without striking, and she learns that good positioning can move an opponent where she wants without anything near her maximum output, an important realization for someone afraid of hurting people. She still hates being put on the mat to prove the lesson; her durability makes the fall painless, but her pride less so, and every time Gunhead says "Again," she stares at the ceiling for a moment and then gets up, never refusing.

Toward the end he moves them onto handling armed opponents, where Narumi's bad habit is relying on her durability. Gunhead corrects that at once, since being hard to injure is no reason to let an armed opponent connect when she has no way of knowing whether an unfamiliar weapon or Quirk could bypass Mass Inversion. Made to treat the practice blade as genuinely dangerous, she learns to move off the line of attack, control the weapon arm, break balance, and create space to disarm or take the attacker down, and by the end she is leaning less on strength and more on timing, which is exactly why she picked his agency. Training against each other so often, both improve quickly: Ochaco gets better at creating the opening her touch needs, and Narumi gets better at keeping contact without feeling she has to either release or overpower, their matches turning competitive enough that neither is graceful about losing.

The Hosu Incident

Midway through the internship, Ochaco gets an alarming message from Midoriya with little more than his location in Hosu, and later learns he, Iida, and Todoroki were caught up in the Hero Killer incident. Narumi is nearby when Ochaco calls to check on them; she does not crowd her or ask questions, but once Ochaco hangs up she asks whether everyone is alive, and when Ochaco confirms it she just nods, says okay, and goes back to what she was doing, though the tension leaves her shoulders. When Gunhead then catches Ochaco on her phone and teases her about talking to her boyfriend, leaving her flustered, Narumi watches her flail to deny it and looks away, fully intending to remember it.

Growing Closer to Ochaco

The two do not become inseparable over the internship, since their personalities are too different for that, but a week of training together shifts things. Narumi finds that Ochaco's kindness is not performative: she does not pry when Narumi goes quiet or point out every small sign of affection. Ochaco, in turn, gets better at reading Narumi's flatter reactions, learning that a quiet "yeah" means real agreement, that sitting beside her without complaint usually means she wants the company, and that an eye roll paired with staying put rarely means she wants anyone to leave. Narumi starts to like her, which creates a new problem, because she understands in theory how acquaintances become friends but has no idea how to cross that line without making it embarrassing. Several times she considers asking Ochaco to do something after training and overthinks it until the moment passes; the closest she manages is waiting by the exit and asking whether Ochaco is taking the same train back, then nodding as if that was all she wanted before walking to the station with her anyway.

By the end she leaves Gunhead's agency with a much stronger grasp of close-range control. She is still cautious about striking with real strength, but that no longer leaves her with only avoidance or overwhelming force, since she has begun building a middle ground out of grips, throws, restraint, positioning, and controlled takedowns. Where Ochaco's new fighting spirit is obvious to the whole class on their return, Narumi's change is quieter: she stands differently in close-range exercises, stops retreating the moment someone enters grappling distance, and increasingly controls a grip instead of just pulling away, having started to learn that physical contact does not have to mean losing control. She also leaves a little closer to Ochaco, seeking her out on purpose rather than merely tolerating her, sitting beside her at lunch, occasionally waiting after class, and asking whether she wants to play a game or study while phrasing it as though she happened to be doing it anyway. She wants to be Ochaco's friend; she just has not worked out how to say so without wanting to crawl out of her own skin.

Final Exams Arc

One week before the end-of-term tests, Aizawa reminds Class 1-A to prepare for both the written and practical portions of their finals, and several students admit they have not kept up during the semester.

Studying

When Yaoyorozu offers to tutor a study group and Kirishima considers joining her, Bakugo takes offense and insists he can tutor Kirishima himself. Narumi immediately says she will join them, and Bakugo refuses at first, arguing she does not need his help. She does well in mathematics, physics, biology, and English but tends to neglect memorization-heavy subjects like history and civics, and studying with others would force her to stay on material she finds boring. Her reasons are not entirely academic, though: she has grown increasingly aware of how differently Bakugo and Kirishima approach becoming heroes, Bakugo with an almost unreasonable faith in reaching the top and Kirishima with a sincerity she finds embarrassing but envies, both of them far more comfortable admitting what they want than she is. She would never say so, but part of her wants to be around that confidence in the hope some of it rubs off. Kirishima backs her request, and once he again calls Yaoyorozu virtuous for helping several people at once, Bakugo reluctantly lets Narumi in.

Over the next week the three study together several times, including at a family restaurant after school. Bakugo turns out to be an abrasive but effective tutor who refuses guessed answers and makes Kirishima work through problems until he understands them; Narumi helps Kirishima with math and science while Bakugo pushes her through the dates, legal procedures, and civics she would otherwise ignore. His methods irritate her, but she comes away with a real appreciation for his intelligence and work ethic, since he complains constantly yet takes Kirishima's improvement seriously and never stops explaining just because it takes longer to land. Kirishima affects her differently: he is not embarrassed about needing help, asks questions freely, celebrates when he finally gets something right, and treats difficulty as normal rather than shameful, which Narumi, who has spent much of her life avoiding any visible struggle, quietly notices. She keeps attending even after reviewing what she actually needed, telling herself the extra study cannot hurt, though by then the material is only part of why she keeps showing up.

Written Examination

Narumi passes the written portion without difficulty, doing especially well in mathematics, physics, biology, and English, with the study sessions lifting her weaker history and civics results even if those stay her least favorite subjects. Kirishima passes too and credits Bakugo's tutoring, which Bakugo waves off as the bare minimum expected.

Practical Examination: vs. Midnight

For the practical, Class 1-A changes into costume and learns from Principal Nezu that the exam is now simulated fights against the teachers rather than a robot battle: most students pair off against one faculty member, with thirty minutes to either cuff their teacher or get one student through the escape gate, and the teachers wearing weighted handicaps. Narumi is put on the only three-person team, with Sero and Mineta, against Midnight; she is fine working with Sero, whose Tape gives them mobility, and far less pleased about Mineta, though she admits Pop Off could restrict Midnight's movement. Watching earlier matches, she pays attention to Kirishima and Sato against Cementoss, who lose by trying to smash through every wall he raises until their Quirks give out, and she notes that they relied on being strong enough to break the first wall without ever asking whether breaking through was actually getting them closer to the gate.

When her own match starts, Narumi immediately recognizes that her durability does nothing against Somnambulist, since it cannot keep her from passing out after inhaling enough sleeping gas. The team plans to avoid direct combat, with Sero moving them between buildings, Narumi giving aerial support and retrieving anyone separated, and Mineta laying spheres to slow pursuit. Sero pulls them above the street, but Midnight anticipates the route and releases gas ahead of them; Sero redirects the others clear but inhales enough to fall unconscious, and Narumi catches him before he lands. Midnight then uses her whip and gas to drive Narumi off, carries the sleeping Sero to the gate, and waits. Narumi stays airborne outside the thickest gas but cannot safely reach Sero or rush the gate through the cloud while carrying Mineta. Mineta appears to panic and flee, drawing Midnight after him, but Narumi realizes he is deliberately pulling her away from Sero and the gate; he covers his mouth and nose with a length of Sero's tape, charges with Grape Rush, and when Midnight tries to whip him, Narumi catches the weapon from outside the cloud so she cannot reel him in. Mineta buries Midnight and the pavement in spheres to pin her, and with Midnight stuck, Narumi grabs Sero and carries him through the gate while Mineta follows. The team clears it in time. Narumi and Mineta both pass, Mineta for spotting the opening and immobilizing Midnight and Narumi for recognizing the danger of the Quirk, protecting Sero, backing Mineta's plan, and carrying their teammate out rather than trying to overpower Midnight; she is irritated that Mineta's plan was what carried them but cannot deny it worked. Sero fails his individual grade despite being carried through, having slept through most of the exam, which he finds more embarrassing than a full team loss.

Midoriya and Bakugo vs. All Might

Back in the monitoring area, Narumi watches the final match against All Might. It is obvious at once that Midoriya and Bakugo cannot cooperate, with Bakugo hitting Midoriya before All Might even reaches them and repeatedly trying to face their teacher alone, and All Might overwhelms both despite his restraints. She becomes visibly concerned when All Might sends Bakugo through the street, as does Kirishima, though neither leaves the room. The two eventually return with a coordinated plan, Bakugo attacking from behind while Midoriya fires one of his Grenadier Bracers at close range, which surprises Narumi, since she did not expect Bakugo to let Midoriya touch his equipment. All Might catches them again and destroys the remaining bracers, but when Bakugo is knocked out near the exit, Midoriya turns back from a spot where he could have escaped alone, strikes All Might, retrieves Bakugo, and carries him through the gate to pass. Narumi does not know what was said or what finally made Bakugo cooperate; she only sees that they started refusing to work together and finished relying on each other, and their relationship stays difficult for her to understand.

Final Results

Back in homeroom, Kirishima, Sato, Kaminari, and Mina fail their matches, and Sero takes a failing individual grade for sleeping through his team's win. The students assume failing the practical means missing the summer training camp, and Kirishima is noticeably discouraged to learn his written work will not qualify him. Narumi awkwardly tries to reassure him, pointing out that Cementoss was a terrible matchup for him and Sato and that one failed strategy does not erase his progress; when it gets more sincere than she is comfortable with, she offers to bring him something back from camp if he has to stay behind, and when he asks what, she suggests a rock, which gets him to laugh. Aizawa then reveals that everyone is going to camp after all, the exclusion threat having been a ruse to make them take the exams seriously, though the students who failed will get remedial lessons on the trip, which cheers the four up immediately.

Kiyashi Ward Shopping Mall

Aizawa hands out a supply list for the forest camp, and Hagakure suggests the class shop together at Kiyashi Ward Shopping Mall. When Kirishima invites both Bakugo and Narumi, Bakugo refuses immediately; Narumi does not. By now Kirishima has started including her alongside Bakugo without making anything of it, and she has mostly stopped pretending she only spends time with them because circumstances keep throwing them together, so she accepts without needing convincing. The next day she joins most of the class at the mall, buying toiletries, sunscreen, spare clothes, and the concentrated nutritional products she needs for prolonged Mass Inversion use, and spends part of the outing with several of the girls. Crowded public spaces can still make her uncomfortable, especially when people recognize her from the Festival or her acting career, but she never considers leaving early, since being out with her classmates has started to feel normal. More than that, she had been given the choice to come and simply chosen yes, which for Narumi is a small but meaningful change.

Relationships / 人間関係

Eijiro Kirishima

One of the first classmates to get past her defenses. His warmth and blunt loyalty remind her of her older brother, which makes her softer toward him than she would like. She still calls him annoying and rolls her eyes, but the words lose their edge. He includes her without making her explain why she wants to be included, and that makes him nearly impossible to push away.

Katsuki Bakugo

He unsettles her. Loud, aggressive, and exhausting, but also brilliant, driven, and completely unwilling to shrink his ambition to make anyone comfortable, which she quietly envies. Around him her boredom mask slips. He notices when she is holding back and pushes straight against it, refusing to treat her like a pretty, distant former child actress. It frustrates her, mostly because he is not always wrong.
